AETHER BRAIN - UNREAL ENGINE 5.8 VERSION
=========================================

You need (once):
  1. Unreal Engine 5.8            (Epic Games Launcher > Unreal Engine > Library)
  2. Visual Studio 2022           (Visual Studio Installer > "Game development with C++")

Steps:
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere short, like C:\AetherBrain-Unreal  (right-click the zip > Extract All)
  2. Open  unreal\AetherBrainViewer\AetherBrainViewer.uproject   (double-click)
       - It asks "build the missing modules?"  ->  Yes. First time takes a few minutes.
       - The editor opens. It makes its own glow materials and an empty map. Nothing to click.
       - First start only: the editor compiles the project's shaders (look for "Compiling Shaders"
         in the bottom right corner). Wait until it says nothing before you press Play; it can
         take several minutes, and a big brain is slow until it is done.
  3. Press Play (the green triangle). You see a dark space and a status line at the top.
  4. In the Aether Brain desktop app: press the  a  key, switch "Unreal companion" ON.
       Your brain appears in Unreal. Fly: hold right mouse + WASD.

Use Aether Brain and this viewer from the SAME release (both 0.9.2 or later, same version number).
A very big vault is cut to its most important 12,000 nodes so that Unreal stays fast; the Aether
Brain panel (a key) and the line at the bottom of the viewer say how much of it you are seeing.

Want a standalone  AetherBrainViewer.exe  (no editor needed)? It is the better way to look at a big vault.
  - Close the editor, then double-click  unreal\PackageWindows.bat
  - 10 to 30 minutes the first time. The program ends up in  unreal\Packaged\Windows\

If something fails, copy the red error text word for word and send it back.

Honest status: this C++ was written without Unreal installed and has never been compiled
in a real 5.8 editor. The first compile may show an error. That is expected; send it back
and it gets fixed. More in docs\UNREAL.md. The optional Niagara effects (key N) need five
assets made by hand in the editor: docs\UNREAL-NIAGARA.md.
