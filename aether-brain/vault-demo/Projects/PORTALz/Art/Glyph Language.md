# Glyph Language

The builders wrote in light. Every emissive inlay on a [[PORTALz]] ruin uses the same small alphabet so the world reads as one civilization.

- **Strokes:** straight cuts and full circles only, never freehand curves (circles carry energy, per [[Shape Language]])
- **Rhythm:** glyphs sit in groups of three with a wide gap, so a wall reads as sentences at 200 m
- **Color:** cold cyan when powered, dead bronze when not; the only saturated color on screen ([[Ancient High-Tech Aesthetic]])
- **Damage:** a cracked panel splits a glyph cleanly; the channel stays pristine on both halves

Material side lives in [[Glyph Emissive Material]].
