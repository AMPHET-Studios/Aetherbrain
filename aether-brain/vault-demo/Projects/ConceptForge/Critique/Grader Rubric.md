# Grader Rubric

What the offline grader in [[ConceptForge]] measures and how each drill weights it. The answer to [[Question - Can value studies be graded automatically]]: yes, as a warm-up checker, never as a judge.

| Drill | Value range | Structure | Edges | Line economy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Value study | 40% | 40% | 20% | — |
| Gesture | — | — | 20% | 80% |
| Thumbnail comps | 30% | 50% ([[Composition]]) | 20% | — |

Scores are for trends across weeks, not verdicts on a single drawing ([[Deliberate Practice]]).
