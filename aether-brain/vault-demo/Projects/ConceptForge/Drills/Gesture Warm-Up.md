# Gesture Warm-Up

The first drill of every session in [[ConceptForge]]: twenty poses at 30 seconds each.

- One continuous line of action first, then the big masses ([[Gesture Drawing]])
- Pressure curve set light so long strokes stay thin and fast
- Grader scores line economy: fewer, longer strokes beat many short ones
- Anything under 10 strokes per pose counts as a win
