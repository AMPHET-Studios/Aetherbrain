# Aether Brain Launch Plan

Goal: 200 people using [[Aether Brain]] on their own vaults by the end of the year, and at least 20 of them telling me what's broken. #writing

## Principles
- Show, don't pitch: a 30-second flythrough of a real vault beats any feature list
- The demo vault must feel like a real person's brain — it *is* mine, lightly curated
- Work in public; like [[Evergreen Notes|evergreen notes]], the story improves each time I revisit it

## Checklist
- [x] Demo vault with all four [[Maps of Content|maps]]
- [x] Capture flythrough footage at 60 fps
- [ ] Landing page: one looping video and a download button
- [ ] Devlog post: "Notes as neurons"
- [ ] Private beta: 30 invites, half artists, half note-taking nerds
- [ ] Resolve the blockers in [[Aether Brain#Open Questions]]
- [ ] Case-study page alongside the [[Concept Art Portfolio 2026]]

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Large vaults (10k+ notes) choke the layout | Barnes–Hut in a worker; cap edges drawn |
| "Pretty but useless" | Ship search, backlinks and unlinked mentions on day one |
| Launch eats all art time | Timebox to two evenings a week, protected by my [[Career]] priorities |

## After launch
Collect feedback in one place, fix the top three complaints, then write an honest retrospective.
