# Quote - Luhmann on surprise

My paraphrase (not his wording) of an idea from [[Niklas Luhmann]]'s 1981 essay on communicating with slip-boxes:

> A slip-box is only worth talking to if it can surprise you. If it just hands back what you put in, it's a filing cabinet, not a partner.

This is the whole case for linking in a [[Zettelkasten]]: connections made months apart collide and produce something neither note said alone. It's also [[Aether Brain#Why|why I'm building Aether Brain]] — a 3D graph becomes a surprise generator when the [[Force-Directed Layout|layout]] pulls distant ideas close.
