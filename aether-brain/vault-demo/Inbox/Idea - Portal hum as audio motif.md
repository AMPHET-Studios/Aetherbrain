# Idea - Portal hum as audio motif

#idea #portal-game What if the portal's hum *is* the game's musical motif? A low drone, two notes a fifth apart, that drifts into tune as the player restores power. The [[Monolith Generator Asset]] carries the same hum an octave down, so players learn to follow the sound through the ruins.

- Ties into [[Game Feel]]: the hum swells on approach and ducks during traversal
- Needs real [[Sound Design]] help — I know just enough to be dangerous
- Cheap wayfinding for the [[Portal Game Vertical Slice]]: no UI markers needed
