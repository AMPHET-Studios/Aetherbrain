# Mira Okafor

Art director and my portfolio mentor. Fifteen years in environment concept art across console and indie studios; now leads art direction on an unannounced project and mentors a handful of juniors on the side. We meet once a month.

## How she reviews
- Starts from across the room: thumbnail size first, then full screen
- Asks "what's the one idea here?" before commenting on anything else
- Is ruthless about Value Structure — if a piece fails in grayscale, she won't discuss color
- Always ends with one thing to keep and one thing to kill

## Feedback Log
- **2026-09-20** — Keyframe 2 (gate at night): the portal glow fights the moonlit sky. Drop the sky a full value and let the gate own the light. Kill the second figure; it splits the focal point. Keep the roots framing the left edge.
