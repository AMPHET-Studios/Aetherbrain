# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator (1890–1974). At MIT he built the differential analyzer, an influential analog computer, and during World War II he led the US Office of Scientific Research and Development.

## "As We May Think"
His July 1945 essay in *The Atlantic* worried that research was producing more knowledge than anyone could find again. His proposed answer was the [[Memex]]: a desk-sized personal library where you could link items into associative trails, the way the mind actually jumps between ideas.

## Ideas that stuck
- Filing by category and alphabet is unnatural; association is how we think
- Trails should be saveable and shareable — knowledge as something you hand on
- A machine can act as an extension of personal memory

## Influence
The essay is a common ancestor of hypertext, the web, and today's tools for thought — the lineage that writers like [[Andy Matuschak]] still build on.
