# James Gurney

American artist and author, best known for *Dinotopia* (1992), an illustrated world where humans and dinosaurs live side by side. He also wrote two of the most useful instruction books on my shelf: *Imaginative Realism* (2009) and *Color and Light* (2010).

## What I learn from him
- **Imagination grounded in observation.** Fantastical subjects painted with the logic of real light, so they feel observed rather than invented
- **Maquettes and reference.** He builds small models and poses reference to solve lighting for scenes that can't exist
- **Plein-air practice.** Small outdoor studies, often in gouache or watercolor, keep the eye honest
- **Explaining the why.** His writing breaks down how light behaves instead of handing out formulas

## Notes shaped by his work
- [[Color and Light]] — named after his book
- [[Value Structure]] — his small studies group values simply and clearly
- [[Environmental Storytelling]] — Dinotopia is world-building through images: architecture, costume and daily routines imply a whole culture
