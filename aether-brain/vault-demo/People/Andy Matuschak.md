# Andy Matuschak

Software engineer and researcher working on tools for thought — software that helps people think and learn better. Earlier in his career he worked on iOS at Apple and led research and development at Khan Academy.

## Ideas I've taken from him
- **Evergreen notes** — his public, densely linked working notes popularized the idea for me; see [[Evergreen Notes]]
- **Working notes in public** — his notes site is itself a demonstration of a Zettelkasten-like practice that anyone can browse
- **The mnemonic medium** — with Michael Nielsen he built *Quantum Country*, an essay on quantum computing with [[Spaced Repetition]] prompts embedded in the text, so reading and remembering happen together
- **Tools for thought** — asking what new kinds of thinking a medium makes possible, in the lineage of the [[Memex]]
