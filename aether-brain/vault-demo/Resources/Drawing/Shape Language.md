# Shape Language

The emotional vocabulary of basic shapes. Viewers read it long before they read detail.

| Shape | Reads as | Portal-game use |
|---|---|---|
| Circle | friendly, soft, whole | the portal ring: unity, cycles |
| Square | stable, heavy, trustworthy | monoliths, terraces, masonry |
| Triangle | dynamic, dangerous, directional | energy vents, blades, broken edges |

## Combining
Pure shapes are dull. A design usually has a dominant shape plus a secondary accent: a square monolith with triangular cuts says "ancient, and dangerous". Keep the ratio uneven — something like 70/20/10 — so one idea wins.

## Rhythm
Alternate straights and curves, simple and complex areas. The same rhythm drives good [[Gesture Drawing]]: a straight on one side of a limb against a curve on the other.

## Where it applies
- Readable outlines — [[Silhouette Design]]
- Picture-level design — [[Composition]] uses big shapes the same way
- The whole look of the game — the [[Ancient High-Tech Aesthetic]] is basically "square stone, circular energy"
- 3D: primary, secondary and tertiary forms in [[Hard-Surface Modeling]]

## Exercise
Design the same object three times — once circle-dominant, once square, once triangle — and ask a friend which one is the villain's.
