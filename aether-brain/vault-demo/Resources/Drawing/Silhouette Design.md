# Silhouette Design

If a design can't be recognized as a solid black shape, it isn't finished. The silhouette is the first thing a player reads — often at a distance, in fog, while moving.

## The test
Fill the design in black on a white background and ask:
- Can I tell what it is?
- Can I tell which way it faces and what it's doing?
- Is there a clear primary shape with secondary interest?

This is the [[Value Structure#Squint Test]] turned all the way up.

## Techniques
- Vary the outline: big simple masses plus a few sharp protrusions
- Use negative space — gaps between limbs, holes through structures
- Exaggerate the pose along the [[Gesture Drawing#Line of Action]]
- Let [[Shape Language]] tell the story: a triangular cut reads as danger even in pure black

## In games
Team Fortress 2 is the famous example — every class is identifiable from its silhouette alone. For the portal game, the [[Monolith Generator Asset]] has to separate from dense jungle: a clean vertical against chaotic foliage. Readable shapes also support [[Game Feel]], because players react faster to what they can parse instantly.

## Workflow
Explore silhouettes first, as a page of black [[Thumbnailing|thumbnails]], then pick the strongest few to develop with interior detail.
