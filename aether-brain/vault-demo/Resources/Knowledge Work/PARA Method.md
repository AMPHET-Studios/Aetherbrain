# PARA Method

Tiago Forte's system for organizing information by **actionability** rather than by topic. Everything fits into one of four buckets:

| Bucket | Definition | Example here |
|---|---|---|
| Projects | Short-term effort with a goal and an end | [[Portal Game Vertical Slice]] |
| Areas | Ongoing responsibility with a standard to maintain | [[Creative Practice]] |
| Resources | Topics of interest, useful someday | drawing, graphics, game-dev notes |
| Archive | Anything inactive from the other three | finished projects, retired sites |

## Why it works
"Where does this go?" becomes "what will I use this for next?" — a much easier question at capture time. Notes flow between buckets as life changes: a resource becomes project material, a finished project gets archived.

## How I bend it
- Folders follow PARA; *meaning* follows links — see [[Zettelkasten#Links over folders]]
- Maps sit on top of both as navigation — [[Maps of Content]]
- Resource notes get compressed over time with [[Progressive Summarization]], another of Forte's ideas
- The front door of this vault, [[Home]], is organized the same way

## Failure mode
Treating PARA as a topic taxonomy. "Drawing" isn't an area; "keeping my drawing skills sharp" is.
