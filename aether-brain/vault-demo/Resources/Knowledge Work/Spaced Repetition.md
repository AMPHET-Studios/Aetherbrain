# Spaced Repetition

Review information at increasing intervals, right before you'd forget it. Each successful recall pushes the next review further out; each failure brings it back.

## Background
- Hermann Ebbinghaus mapped the forgetting curve in the 1880s
- The Leitner box (1970s) made it physical: cards you get right move to boxes you review less often
- SuperMemo's SM-2 algorithm became the basis for Anki; newer schedulers such as FSRS model memory more directly

## Why it works
Retrieval is the workout. Re-reading *feels* productive; recalling *is* productive — the testing effect. Spacing makes each retrieval just effortful enough to strengthen the memory.
