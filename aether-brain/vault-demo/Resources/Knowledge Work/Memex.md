# Memex

The imagined device from [[Vannevar Bush]]'s 1945 essay "As We May Think" in *The Atlantic*: a desk that stores a person's books, records and correspondence on microfilm, with screens for reading and a mechanism for tying any two items together.

## Associative trails
Bush's key insight was that the mind works by association, not by index. The memex let you build **trails** — chains of linked documents — name them, share them with others, and follow them again later. The trail was the product, not the storage.

## Legacy
The essay influenced Douglas Engelbart and Ted Nelson, and through them hypertext and the web. Every wikilink is a tiny trail segment. Writers on tools for thought, like [[Andy Matuschak]], are still working on the same question: how does a medium change what a person can think?
