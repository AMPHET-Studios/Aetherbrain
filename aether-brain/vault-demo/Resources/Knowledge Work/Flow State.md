# Flow State

Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi's name for total absorption in an activity: time distorts, self-consciousness drops away, and the work becomes rewarding in itself.

## Conditions
- Challenge roughly matched to skill — too easy is boredom, too hard is anxiety
- Clear goals for the session
- Immediate feedback from the work itself
- No interruptions (the phone lives in another room)

## My reliable entries
Thumbnailing is my easiest way in: small, fast, low stakes, instant feedback. Twenty minutes of little frames usually carries me straight into a painting session. Code gets there too, but only when the next step is concrete — "fix the pulse speed", not "improve the shader".

## Watch-outs
- Flow isn't the same as practice. It feels great partly because it's comfortable; [[Deliberate Practice]] often isn't.
- It depends on energy. I can't get there tired — see [[Health and Energy]].

## Games do this on purpose
Good games tune difficulty and feedback to keep players in the channel between boredom and anxiety. [[Game Feel]] is the moment-to-moment side of that same design problem.
