# Ancient High-Tech Aesthetic

The visual identity of the portal game: a civilization that mastered energy long ago, whose machines still hum under a rainforest that has swallowed them. Sci-fi precision inside dark-fantasy age.

## Core contrasts
- **Mass vs. line:** huge weathered stone masses carrying razor-thin glowing channels
- **Warm vs. cold:** humid golden light, deep green shadow, cold cyan energy — see [[Color and Light]]
- **Age vs. function:** moss, roots and cracks everywhere except the energy seams, which stay pristine
- **Order vs. growth:** perfect geometry slowly being pried apart by roots

## Shape rules
Square and rectangular masses for architecture, circles for anything that carries energy (portals, rings, lenses), triangles only where something is broken or dangerous — straight from [[Shape Language]].

## Material palette
| Material | Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Basalt and dark stone | ~70% | rough, wet streaks, carved panels |
| Oxidized bronze | ~20% | clamps, rings, inlays; green patina |
| Crystal and emissive | ~10% | the only saturated color on screen |

All of it built from a small set of [[PBR Materials]] and trim sheets.

## Storytelling
The ruins should imply three eras — the builders, the fall, the reclamation. See [[Environmental Storytelling]].
