# Health and Energy

Art and code are both desk sports. This area exists so my body doesn't quietly become the bottleneck. #health

## Standards
- 7.5+ hours of sleep on most nights — details in [[Sleep Hygiene]]
- A walk before the first screen; half my ideas arrive there
- The 20-20-20 rule for eyes: every 20 minutes, look about 20 feet away for 20 seconds
- Wrist check: stretch between drawing sessions, switch mouse hands for code days

## Energy map
| Time | Energy | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| 08–11 | high | painting and hard problems — protected for [[Flow State]] |
| 11–14 | medium | modeling, calls |
| 14–16 | low | admin, inbox processing |
| 19–21 | medium | gesture practice, reading |

## Notes
- Long focused sessions only work when I'm rested; [[Deliberate Practice]] is tiring by design
- Burnout signal: when [[Creative Practice]] feels like a chore three days running, take a full day off
- Sleep is when practice sticks — memory consolidation is half of [[Learning]]
