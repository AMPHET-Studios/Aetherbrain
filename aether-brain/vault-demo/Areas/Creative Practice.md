# Creative Practice

The area that keeps my hands sharp no matter which project is on fire. Standards, not goals — nothing here is ever "finished".

## Standards
| Cadence | Practice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | 20 min of [[Gesture Drawing]] | loosens the hand, trains seeing action |
| Daily | 3–6 [[Thumbnailing|thumbnails]] | idea volume |
| Weekly | One master study, values only | steal structure from the best |
| Monthly | One finished piece | feeds the [[Concept Art Portfolio 2026]] |

## Principles
- Practice the way [[Deliberate Practice]] describes: one weakness per session, feedback at the end
- Protect the warm-up; it's my most reliable door into [[Flow State]]
- Game work counts — painting keyframes for the [[Portal Game Vertical Slice]] is practice with stakes
- Energy is the real budget, not hours — see [[Health and Energy]]

## Current weakness
Edges. Everything I paint is hard-edged, so nothing sits in atmosphere. This month: lost-and-found edge studies from photos of fog in forests, 15 minutes each.
