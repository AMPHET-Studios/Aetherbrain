# Old Portfolio Site

My first portfolio site, live from 2022 until early 2025. Archived here so I remember what not to do.

## What it was
- A single scrolling page with about forty pieces in no particular order
- Student work mixed with client work mixed with fan art
- Huge image files; painfully slow on phones
- No process, no context, no contact button above the fold

## What I learned
- Curation *is* the portfolio. Forty mixed pieces read as "not sure what I want".
- Lead with the job you want: environments and props, not everything you can do.
- Process pages matter as much as finished pieces.
- Load speed is part of the first impression.

## Salvaged
- Three environment pieces worth repainting
- The simple contact-form layout
